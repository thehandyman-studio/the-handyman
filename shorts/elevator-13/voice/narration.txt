This elevator had twelve floors. So why was there a button for thirteen?
Leo was only there to fix a broken elevator. But something about this one felt wrong.
Then the button lit up. The doors closed. And the elevator started going down.
Past the basement. Past the foundations. Much deeper than the building should even allow.
When the doors opened, Leo couldn't believe what he was seeing.
An entire city. Hidden beneath the ground. Empty streets. Thousands of buildings. And lights still glowing in the windows.
Then he noticed something familiar.
One of the buildings was identical to the one above.
And in one of its windows... he saw himself.
Suddenly, the elevator behind him opened.
The button for thirteen was glowing again.
And this time... it was waiting for him.
